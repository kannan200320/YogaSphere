About the Practice

Vinyasa Flow is a highly popular contemporary yoga style characterized by stringing postures together so that you move from one to another seamlessly, using breath control (pranayama). Often referred to as "flow" yoga, Vinyasa classes offer a variety of postures and no two classes are ever exactly alike.

The variable structure of Vinyasa prevents repetitive stress injuries that can occur if you perform the exact same movements every day. It serves as an excellent cardiovascular movement practice while helping build core control, posture resilience, and muscular endurance.

Primary Benefits

  • ✓ Strengthens core muscle walls, upper body joints, and leg stability.
  • ✓ Increases active flexibility and spinal mobility range.
  • ✓ Promotes stress reduction and mental stamina through continuous focus.

What to Expect

Class typically begins with a warm-up, followed by progressively energetic sun salutations and flow sequences. It concludes with cool-down stretching and a deep, quiet savasana (resting posture). Please bring a yoga mat, a hand towel, and a water flask. We recommend wearing comfortable, sweat-wicking clothing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about scheduling, pricing, or bookings? Check out our quick answers below.

What is your cancellation policy? +
You can cancel any booked class up to 12 hours before the start time without losing your session credit. Late cancellations or no-shows will deduct one session from your membership pack.
Do you provide mats and towels? +
We provide high-quality yoga mats and sweat towels free of charge for all studio sessions. You are also welcome to bring your personal mat if you prefer.
What if I am running late for a class? +
To preserve the peaceful atmosphere of the studio, doors close exactly 2 minutes before class begins. Late entry is unfortunately not permitted once the teacher has initiated the warm-up.
Can beginners join Vinyasa Flow? +
Vinyasa Flow is highly dynamic. While beginners are absolutely welcome (our teachers offer modifications), we highly recommend starting with our Hatha Alignment classes to learn basic posture shapes first.
